How it is drawn

Four passes, like a real page

  1. Lay-in

    A light graphite pass blocks in the big shapes first, just like an artist checking proportions.

  2. Contours

    The longest, most important lines go down in ink, traced from the original drawing’s edges.

  3. Hatching

    Thousands of short strokes follow, ordered so the pen moves to the nearest unfinished line.

  4. Tone

    A soft brush sweeps across the page, washing in the artist’s real shading, then the ink settles back.
